## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: CatBall basic pixel-art gameplay field mockup, a static visual study for a small browser dodgeball game.
Create ONE landscape 16:9 image, ideally 1280x720, showing the complete fixed-camera arena. This is the first minimal playable-screen art study, not a poster, splash screen, infographic, or elaborate illustration.

SCENE AND COMPOSITION:
A simple school sports ground seen in a classic 2D top-down RPG view with front/three-quarter-view chibi sprites. The ground is an axis-aligned rectangle with parallel edges, NOT an isometric diamond or perspective trapezoid. A large quiet muted medium-blue rectangular playing court fills roughly the central 70% of image width and 66% of image height. An off-white thin pixel boundary, a restrained center line and a small center circle indicate the court; no busy basketball markings. A broad brick-red rectangular running-track band completely surrounds the court on ALL FOUR sides, with one or two restrained lane lines and sufficient space for throwers on every side. Narrow low-detail green grass margins surround the track. Only a small suggestion of cream school-building facade and a few simple blocky trees along the distant TOP border. Most of the screen is usable clear court. No obstacles inside it. Keep all four court edges fully visible.

Each character should read like a deliberately crafted roughly 32x32-pixel sprite enlarged cleanly, occupy about 6-7% of screen height, have a large readable head and compact body, clear dark outline and 2-3 shade clusters per material. Do not render fur hairs or tiny clothing details. No large illustration characters.

The four outside characters are oriented approximately toward the arena and remain entirely on the red track; the main cat is the only schoolchild inside the blue court. The intent is sprite size and basic recognition, not precise miniature text: omit illegible school lettering.

READABILITY TEST OBJECTS:
Inside the court, spaciously separated from the main player, place ONE small uncostumed pale-yellow duck with an orange bill and feet, natural two-legged animal posture; ONE small bright coral/orange dodgeball with a simple pale highlight; and ONE larger golden-orange dodgeball with about 2.5 times the small ball's diameter. Use distinct clear silhouettes and tiny hard-edged oval ground shadows. Keep the player, duck and both balls separated with generous empty room. No explosions, particle clouds, speed-line clutter or expression bubbles in this basic field draft.

PIXEL STYLE:
Crisp economical authentic low-resolution sprite-and-tile pixel art, inspired by simple handheld-era overhead RPG sprites. Consistent visible square pixel grid, nearest-neighbor-like hard edges, compact color palette, mostly flat colors, just a few pixel shade clusters, minimal ground texture. No photographic texture, no 3D rendering, no smooth gradients, no blur, no anti-aliased painterly edges, no cinematic lighting or bloom. Court should be calm and slightly desaturated so white-blue characters and orange balls stand out. The cast must have recognizably different head/ear/tail silhouettes despite matching uniforms.
No Pokemon characters, no copied game logos, no watermark. No HUD, text, title, labels, legends, menus, borders or presentation panels: just the actual simple game field sample filling the image.
